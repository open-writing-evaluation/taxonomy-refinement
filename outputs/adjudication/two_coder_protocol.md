# Two-coder protocol for the T3 validity gold

Two coders produce the final-version gold for the fixed subset H (150
test-set edits; ids in `h_ids.json`).

1. **Coders.** Coder 1 is the taxonomy designer. Coder 2 is an expert coder
   **not involved in the taxonomy's design**. Both adjudicate the same 150
   items under the written T3 guideline, each in an independently shuffled
   item order (coder 2's copy `h_T3_sheet_c2.xlsx` is generated by
   `scripts/75_make_c2_sheet.py`; the H selection itself is never
   touched).
2. **Independence.** Both coders adjudicate blind to model outputs and to
   each other, from the written T3 guideline alone (rules as in
   `coder_instructions.md`). Coder 2 receives only the guideline, the
   calibration sheet (10 practice items from D_ref, not from H, not
   scored), and the blank sheet. Guideline-wording questions are answered
   by pointing to guideline text and are logged below. No item is
   discussed until both coders' sheets are complete.
3. **Reported agreement.** Expert–expert agreement (composite %, Cohen's
   kappa, and per-layer figures) is computed on the two coders'
   **independent first-pass labels** by `scripts/80_coder_agreement.py
   compare`, before any discussion, and is the figure reported in the
   paper.
4. **Consensus gold.** Items the coders agree on enter the consensus gold
   as labeled, with `gold_alternates` the union of the two coders'
   alternates. Disagreements are resolved by discussion
   (`h_T3_disagreements.csv`). Tie rule, fixed in advance: if discussion
   does not converge, the jointly preferred reading is the primary gold and
   the other coder's reading enters `gold_alternates` (credited only by
   the set-lenient variant V_set). `scripts/80_coder_agreement.py
   build-consensus` writes `h_T3_sheet_consensus.xlsx`; `85_validity.py`
   then scores r3 (native) and r2 (--crosswalk) against it.
